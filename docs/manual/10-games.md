# 10. Games

Games are in item 7 on the main menu.

- Hisingen Hustle: a trading game. Buy low, sell high and stay off the
  bridge. Your best scores go on the high-score list, one line per player.
- Hisingen Turf Wars: take on the other callers and rule the streets.

Games run in the same terminal and end when you press Q. Scores are shown
in Fame & Glory under BBS Records.
