# 3. Getting around

The main menu has a number for each part. Press the number, no Enter
needed. Upper and lower case keys both work.

  1 Boards   2 Wall   3 News   4 Fame & Glory   5 Mail
  6 My Account   7 Games   8 Files

A * next to an item means something is new there since your last call.

Keys that work in most places:
- ?  Help for the screen you are on. It also points to this manual
  (My Account, Manual).
- Q  Back one step. From the main menu it logs you out.
- The top bar shows shortcuts to jump straight to another part:
  B Boards, M Main menu, P Mail, W Wall, G Goodbye (log out).

Some screens are special. Not every menu has every key, because each part
of the BBS does a different job: a file list needs keys to upload and
flag files, a thread needs keys to reply, a game has its own controls.
The keys that work on a screen are listed on the screen itself, and ? shows
the full list. When a key does nothing, you are on a screen that does not
use it.

Writing longer text (board posts and mail):
- Type your text line by line.
- A single . on a line of its own finishes and sends it.
- /cancel stops at any prompt.
- Posts, edits and deletions can take about a minute to show up for others.

The Wall is different. A wall message is one short line, at most 140
characters, sent with Enter. There is no . to finish and no multi-line text.
Use Boards or Mail for anything longer.

If a screen looks wrong, it is usually the terminal size. 80 columns by
25 rows or more is best; narrower screens work with shorter layouts.
