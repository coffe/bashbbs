# 15. Etiquette and the rules

This is a small place run by hand. A few rules keep it good:

- Be kind. Disagree as much as you like, but about the idea, not the person.
- No spam, no ads, no flooding.
- Only share files you have the right to share. No malware, no illegal
  material.
- Do not try to break in or overload the BBS. Finding a bug and telling the
  sysop is welcome; abusing one is not.
- Private boards and mail are private. Do not paste them elsewhere.
- The sysop can remove posts and files and, if needed, accounts.

Questions, ideas, bug reports or just a hello: write to the sysop in Mail.
Make yourself at home.
