# 2. What is a BBS?

BBS means Bulletin Board System. Before the web, people dialled into a
computer with a modem, read and wrote messages, played games and swapped
files. Only one or a few people could be on at a time, and everybody knew
who everybody was.

BashBBS is the same idea today. You log in over SSH, you get menus you
drive with single keys, and you share the place with a small crowd instead
of the whole internet.

What makes it different from social media:
- It is small on purpose. It runs on a Raspberry Pi Zero W, the original
  one, so it is slow in places and proud of it.
- There are no feeds, no likes and no ads. You read what you choose to read.
- The sysop is a real person you can write to. Mail and private boards are
  only shown to the people they are meant for, but the sysop has
  administrative access to keep the system running, so do not put secrets
  in them.

Everything here is text. Press ? on almost any screen for help, and read
chapter 3 next to learn the keys.
