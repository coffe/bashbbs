One small step for a small computer!

BashBBS runs on a Raspberry Pi Zero W. The original one. Not the Zero 2 W. Nope.
The little old one!

It's built almost entirely with tools that come baked into Debian. No fancy
frameworks, no heavy dependencies.
Well, except Fail2ban. That's not baked in.

Anyway...

This is very much a work in progress, and you're very much invited to poke
around, break things (gently!), and help make it better.

Please tell me what you think!

Found a bug? Let me know.
Got an idea? I'd love to hear it.
Wish there was a feature? Make a wish!
Just want to say hi? Even better!


Here's a quick guide to finding your way around.

The Main Menu

1) Boards – Public and private discussions. Drop by The Pub for a chat, share
interesting links in Shared News, or post upcoming events in What's up?!

2) Wall – Leave a short greeting for everyone. 140 characters, make them count!

3) News – Automatically collected and curated news from around the world.
Read-only, but you're welcome to discuss anything over on the boards.

4) Fame & Glory – Meet the users, see who's been calling, and check out the BBS
records.

5) Mail – Send and receive private messages with other users.

6) My Account – Check your profile, manage SSH keys and change your settings.

A Few Things Worth Knowing

- Press "?" for help on almost any screen.
- Press "Q" to go back one step.
- The top bar has shortcuts for Boards, Main, Mail, Wall and Goodbye. These work
in most menus.
- Uppercase and lowercase keys both work.
- When writing a post or message, enter a single "." on its own line to
finish.
- Posts, edits and deletions may take about a minute to appear.

You can start your own private board, invite other users and have your own
little corner of the BBS.
And don't forget to check your mail!

One Last Thing

This is a hobby project, built for the fun of it. Things might change, features
might appear, and occasionally something might break.
If something seems odd, drop me a message.
Make yourself at home, explore a little and say hello!
Let the Beta Bash begin!
