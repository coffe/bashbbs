My Account
Everything about you lives here.

  1 Profile   Your name, your unique ID and when you joined. Other users
              see parts of this in Fame & Glory.
  2 SSH Keys  The keys that let you log in without a password.
              A add a key, R remove one, N give it a name.
              You can have several keys, for example one per computer.
  3 Settings  C turns colour on or off. N turns the reply-mail reminder on
              or off.

Adding a second key before removing the first is the safe order, so you do
not lock yourself out. If you do get locked out, write to the sysop from
another key or through the contact given on the web page.

  4 Manual    This manual. Also linked from the ? help and the welcome mail.
